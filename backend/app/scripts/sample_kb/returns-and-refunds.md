# Returns and Refunds

## Refund window

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the delivery date. The
delivery date is the date shown as "Delivered" in the order tracking, not the
date the order was placed.

Items must be unused and returned in their original packaging. Items showing
signs of use are assessed individually and may receive a partial refund.

## How refunds are issued

Refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds appear within
5-7 business days of us receiving the returned item. Bank transfers may take up
to 10 business days.

We do not issue refunds as store credit unless the customer specifically asks
for it.

## Return shipping

Return shipping is free for defective items. For change-of-mind returns, the
customer pays return shipping, which is deducted from the refund if a prepaid
label is used.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are processed as a return plus a new order. The replacement ships once
the returned item is scanned by the carrier, not once it arrives at our
warehouse.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Opened consumables and cleaning fluids
- Custom-configured hardware
- Gift cards
- Items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase
