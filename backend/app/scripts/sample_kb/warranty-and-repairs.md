# Warranty and Repairs

## Coverage

All Northwind hardware carries a 2-year limited warranty from the date of
delivery. The warranty covers manufacturing defects: components that fail under
normal use without external cause.

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage, including drops and impacts
- Liquid and water damage
- Normal cosmetic wear such as scratches and scuffs
- Damage from unauthorised repair or modification
- Consumable parts, including batteries after 12 months

## Making a warranty claim

Customers need their order number and a description of the fault. Photographs
help but are not required. We issue a prepaid shipping label for warranty
returns.

Assessment takes 5-7 business days from receipt. If the fault is covered we
repair or replace at our discretion; replacements are new or equivalent
refurbished units.

## Out-of-warranty repairs

Repairs outside the warranty period are quoted before work begins. There is a
$45 assessment fee, waived if the customer proceeds with the repair.

## Battery health

Batteries are considered consumable. A battery holding less than 80% of its
original capacity within the first 12 months is covered; after 12 months it is
not.
