# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery speeds

Standard delivery arrives in 3-5 business days within the continental US.
Express delivery arrives in 1-2 business days. Orders placed after 2pm ET ship
the following business day.

Business days exclude weekends and US federal holidays.

## Where we ship

We ship within the continental United States only. We do not currently ship to
Alaska, Hawaii, US territories, or internationally.

We cannot deliver to PO boxes because our carrier requires a signature for
hardware orders.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed when the order leaves our warehouse. Tracking
information can take up to 4 hours to appear on the carrier's site after the
email is sent.

## Delayed or missing orders

An order is considered delayed if it has not arrived 3 business days past the
estimated delivery date. Weather and carrier disruptions are the most common
cause.

If tracking shows an order as delivered but the customer has not received it, we
open a carrier investigation. Investigations take 3-5 business days.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over $75. Below that it is $8.95. Express
shipping is $24.95 regardless of order value.
